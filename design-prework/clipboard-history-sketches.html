Clipboard history — three directions

Static sketches for a one-week SwiftUI rebuild. Every direction covers the same MVP: mixed text and screenshot history, search, a larger image preview, and picking an item to put it back on the pasteboard so a normal ⌘V pastes it. Sample content is mostly screenshots, because that is what you copy most.

A

Menu-bar list with a preview flyout

The closest to Pastebot: a narrow list drops from the status item, and every row has a ⌘-number. Screenshots get real thumbnails in the row, and a flyout beside the list shows the selected one larger.

Two-pane palette

A Spotlight-sized panel that opens from the menu-bar icon or ⌥⌘V. The search field is focused as soon as it opens. The list is on the left, and the right side always shows a large preview with details. Shown here mid-search, with a screenshot selected.

C

Bottom shelf with Quick Look

Pick

Build B: the two-pane palette

Your main pain is screenshots, and screenshots look alike as small thumbnails. B is the only direction where the larger preview is always on screen, while text, links and search results still read as a normal, scannable list. It also maps cleanly onto the planned stack: one NSPanel that does not take focus, opened from an NSStatusItem or a global hotkey, with a SwiftUI List and a preview view inside. Borrow two details from A: the ⌘1–9 quick-pick numbers and the menu-bar icon as the way in.

In the week-one MVP

  • Poll the pasteboard's changeCount and save text and PNG/TIFF images
  • SQLite rows for metadata and text; image files and thumbnails in Application Support
  • Panel: search field, All / Text / Images chips, list grouped by day, preview pane
  • ↩ or ⌘1–9 writes the item back to the pasteboard and closes the panel, then you press ⌘V yourself
  • ⌘⌫ deletes an item; a cap on history size

Left out on purpose

  • OCR or searching the text inside screenshots
  • Sync, editing, pinned items and filters
  • Auto-paste into the front app (needs Accessibility permission)
  • Source-app icons, if getting the front app is unreliable
  • Rich text and files; plain text and images only
